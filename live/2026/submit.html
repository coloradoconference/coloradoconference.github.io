41st Colorado Conference On

Iterative and Multigrid Methods

Presentation Format

Presentations at the conference are featured in morning and afternoon sessions and are normally 25 minutes in length, including time for questions. Presentations are grouped generally by theme.

How To Participate

To submit an abstract for a presentation, please use the following:

Submit an abstract

At the top of the submission page you can read the submission instructions. There are two options for submission:
  1. An abstract for participation in the regular program (deadline April 3, 2026).
  2. An abstract and paper for the the Student paper competition. See instructions and deadlines for further details. Each submission to the student paper competition will be considered for the regular program -- a separate submission is not needed.

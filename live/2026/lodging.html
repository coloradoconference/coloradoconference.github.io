On-Campus Lodging

We are happy to provide economical lodging in the CU-Boulder residence halls. This is available as a package rate for the week of the conference, from Monday, June 22 through Friday, June 26, for $520.14/person for a single room, and $318.14/person for a shared (double) room, both with shared bathrooms. These rates include all taxes and breakfast for the mornings of June 23 through 26. Additional nights are available for early arrivals on June 20 or 21, or late departures on June 27 or 28 at an additional cost of $103.81/person/night for a single room and $53.31/person/night for a shared room. Note that the conference begins with tutorials and a reception on Sunday, June 21.

{\large ILYA LASHUK \\
{\bf Multilevel methods for Poisson-like equations on the sphere using highly non-uniform meshes}}

Tufts University \\ School of Arts and Sciences \\ Department of Mathematics \\ Bromfield-Pearson Hall \\ 503 Boston Avenue \\ Medford \\ MA 02155
\\
{\tt ilya.lashuk@tufts.edu}\\
James Adler\\
Scott MacLachlan\end{center}

Numerical models of electron transport in 3D include a scattering term
that, in the Fokker-Planck limit, converges to a diffusion operator on
the surface of the unit sphere. Motivated by this example, we have
implemented a finite-element discretization scheme and a multilevel
solver for Poisson-like PDEs on the sphere. We approximate the sphere by
a piecewise-triangular surface, and the triangular faces of the
approximating surface play the role of the finite elements. We are
interested in the problems where the source function (nearly) has a point
singularity and, thus, we use highly nonuniform meshes. Our multilevel
solver is a version of the Fast Adaptive Composite Grid (FAC) method. We
study numerically both the discretization accuracy and the multilevel
solver performance.

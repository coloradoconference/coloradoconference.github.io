{\bf A Multiscale Finite Volume with Oversampling Method to simulate Low-frequency Electromagnetic geophysical responses}}

In Geophysics, simulation of low-frequency Electromagnetic (EM) fields in
highly heterogeneous, anisotropic media is computationally very
expensive. One reason being the multiple length scales that coexist in a
given realistic setting. Discrete models require very fine meshes leading
to solve large linear systems of equations. In this work, we develop a
multiscale finite volume method with oversampling for the quasi-static
Maxwell's equations in the frequency domain. This method reduces the size
of the system of equations to be solved while retaining a good level of
accuracy in the solution. The performance of our method is shown in the
context of an EM geophysical application.

We consider time-dependent PDEs discretized in combined space-time
domains. We first reduce the PDE to a first order system. Very often in
practice, one of the equations of the reduced system involves the
divergence operator (in space-time).
The popular FOSLS (first order system least-squares) method is then
applied modified by keeping the divergence equation as a constraint which
we refer to as CFOSLS (constrained FOSLS). Applying finite elements to
discretize the CFOSLS problem leads to a saddle-point system. To
alleviate the high memory demand of the combined space-time approach (due
to the increased dimension),
we apply element agglomeration AMG upscaling on space-time elements. This
leads to substantially reduced problem sizes with controlled accuracy.
Initial numerical results for model parabolic and scalar hyperbolic
problems illustrate the potential of the method.

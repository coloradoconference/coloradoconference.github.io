Multilevel Monte Carlo is an efficient variance reduction technique to
quantify uncertainties in simulations of subsurface flows with highly
varying, rough coefficients, e.g. in radioactive waste disposal. We study
model elliptic problems of single phase flow in random media described by
correlated lognormal distributions, discretized in space via (standard
and mixed) finite elements. We will demonstrate (theoretically and
numerically) significant gains with respect to standard Monte Carlo,
leading (in the best case) to an asymptotic cost that is proportional to
solving one deterministic PDE.

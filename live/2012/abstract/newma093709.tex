{\bf Physics-based Preconditioners for Ocean Simulation}}

We examine physics-based preconditioners for ocean simulation based on
barotropic-baroclinic splitting. Physics-based preconditioning is a
highly successful approach for multiple time scale problems where an
accurate simulation is desired on the dynamical time scale. Our approach
is a fully implicit, fully coupled time integration of the momentum and
continuity equations of ocean dynamics; thus reducing errors and
increasing stability due to traditional operator splitting. The nonlinear
system is solved via preconditioned Jacobian-free Newton-Krylov, where we
reformulate traditional barotropic-baroclinic splitting as a
preconditioner. Thus the desired solution is timestep converged with
timesteps on the order of the dynamical timescale. We provide numerical
examples to support the study and compare to explicit methods and methods
based on operator splitting.

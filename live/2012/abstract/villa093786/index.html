A block-diagonal algebraic multigrid preconditioner for the Brinkman problem

Dept of Mathematics & Computer Science
Emory University
400 Dowman Dr
Atlanta
GA
30322
uvilla@emory.edu
Panayot S. Vassilevski

The Brinkman model is a unified law governing the flow of a viscous fluid in cavity (Stokes equations) and in porous media (Darcy equations). It was initially proposed as a homogenization technique for the Navier-Stokes equations. Typical applications of this model are in underground water hydrology, petroleum industry, automotive industry, biomedical engineering, and heat pipes modeling. In this talk, we present a novel mixed formulation of the Brinkman problem. Introducing the flow's vorticity as additional unknown, this formulation leads to a uniformly stable and conforming discretization by standard finite element (Nédélec, Raviart-Thomas, piecewise discontinuous). Based on stability analysis of the problem in the norms, we derive a scalable block diagonal preconditioner which is optimal in the constant coefficient case. Such preconditioner is based on the auxiliary space AMG solvers for and problems available in hypre (http://www.llnl.gov/CASC/hypre/). The theoretical results are illustrated by numerical experiments.

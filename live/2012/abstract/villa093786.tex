\newcommand{\curl}{\operatorname{curl}}
\renewcommand{\div}{\operatorname{div}}

The Brinkman model is a unified law governing the flow of a viscous fluid
in cavity (Stokes equations) and in porous media (Darcy equations).
It was initially proposed as a homogenization technique for the Navier-Stokes equations.
Typical applications of this model are in underground water hydrology,
petroleum industry, automotive industry, biomedical engineering, and heat
pipes modeling.
In this talk, we present a novel mixed formulation of the Brinkman
problem. Introducing the flow's vorticity as additional unknown, this
formulation leads to a uniformly stable and conforming discretization by
standard finite element (N\'ed\'elec, Raviart-Thomas, piecewise
discontinuous). Based on stability analysis of the problem in the
$H(\curl)-H(\div)-L^2$ norms, we derive a scalable block diagonal
preconditioner which is optimal in the constant coefficient case. Such
preconditioner is based on the auxiliary space AMG solvers for $H(\curl)$
and $H(\div)$ problems available in hypre
(http://www.llnl.gov/CASC/hypre/). The theoretical results are
illustrated by numerical experiments.\\

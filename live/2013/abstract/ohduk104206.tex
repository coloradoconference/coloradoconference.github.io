{\large Duk-Soon Oh \\
{\bf A Smoother Based on Nonoverlapping Domain Decomposition Methods for H(div) problems}}

216 Johnston Hall \\ Baton Rouge \\ LA 70803
\\
{\tt duksoon@cct.lsu.edu}\\
Susanne C. Brenner\end{center}

A multigrid method for vector field problems discretized with
Raviart-Thomas finite elements is developed and analyzed. The smoother is
based on a modified nonoverlapping domain decomposition methods and
provides the uniform convergence of W-cycle algorithms. Numerical
experiments that support the theory are also presented. This is joint
work with Susanne Brenner.

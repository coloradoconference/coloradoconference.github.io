{\bf Parallel multigrid solvers in coastal ocean simulations}}

Mathematical Sciences \\ Florida Institute of Technology \\ 150 W University Blvd \\ Melbourne \\ FL 32901

{\tt jim@fit.edu}\\
Steven Jachec\end{center}



This work aims to incorporate a parallel multigrid solver for the
nonhydrostatic pressure with application to coastal ocean internal waves
and baroclinic flows. This is accomplished by usage of two packages: the
the Stanford Unstructured Nonhydrostatic Terrain-following Adaptive
Navier-Stokes Simulator (SUNTANS) and the hypre linear solver package
from Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

In this talk we discuss the implementation approach in joining these two
codes, including specific parallel computing issues arising due to the
use of z-level grids in SUNTANS. These grids are unstructured in the
xy-plane, but structured in the z-direction. We include discussion of the
parallel performance of the resulting SUNTANS+BoomerAMG code on the 500+
core cluster at Florida Tech.

We look at using Krylov methods on two grids to compute eigenvalues and
eigenvectors of matrices that come from descretization of a differential
equation. Restarted Arnoldi is applied on the course grid. Then the
eigenvectors are improved on the fine grid. We may also consider multiple
grids and applications to solution of linear equations.

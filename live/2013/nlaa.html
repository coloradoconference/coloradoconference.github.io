Call for Papers - Copper Mountain Special Issue

The 16th Copper Mountain Conference on Multigrid Methods is scheduled from 17 March - 22 March, 2013 (2013).

As in previous odd-numbered years, a Special Issue of Numerical Linear Algebra with Applicationsis planned. This single issue will be dedicated to recent progress in multigrid methods. Submissions are encouraged in all aspects of multigrid methods, including this year's highlighted topics.

Attendees and participants of the conference as well as the general community are invited to submit papers. The papers should meet the formatting and editorial requirements of Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications.

The deadlines, method of submission, guidelines, and any submission requirements will be announced at the conference and posted at this conference website once announced.

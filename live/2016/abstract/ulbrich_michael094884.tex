{\bf Constrained Optimization with Low-Rank Tensors and Applications to Problems with PDEs under Uncertainty}}

Technical University of Munich \\ Department of Mathematics \\ Boltzmannstr 3 \\ 85748 Garching by Muenchen \\ Germany
\\
{\tt mulbrich@ma.tum.de}\\
Sebastian Garreis\end{center}

We present Newton-type methods for inequality constrained nonlinear
optimization using low-rank tensors and apply them to variational
inequalities with several uncertain parameters and to optimal control
problems with PDEs under uncertainty. The developed inexact methods are
tailored to the usage of low-rank tensor arithmetics, which only offer a
limited set of operations and require truncation (rounding) in between.
We show that they can solve huge scale optimization problems with
trillions of unknowns to a good accuracy.

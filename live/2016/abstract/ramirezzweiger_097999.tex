Efficiently solving the eigenvalue form of the radiation transport
equation is an important task in nuclear reactor analysis. Rayleigh
quotient iteration (RQI) offers significantly better iterative
performance relatively to standard solvers used in the industry, such as
power iteration. The downside is that the shifted linear systems that
must be solved within RQI are significantly more challenging than those
encountered in power iteration. In this talk we discuss the difficulties
in solving these shifted linear systems and discuss the use of GMRES in
conjunction with a multigrid in energy preconditioner.

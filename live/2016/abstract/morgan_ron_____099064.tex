{\bf Krylov Methods for Difficult Eigenvalue Problems}}

{\tt Ronald\_Morgan@baylor.edu}\end{center}

We will discuss new Krylov methods for computing eigenvalues and
eigenvectors. These include a multigrid method that improves by doing
much of the computation on coarse grids. Also, a polynomial
preconditioned Arnoldi can significantly speed up difficult eigenvalue
problems. And for very difficult problems, a new method needs two
iterations, but can compute eigenvectors without either restarting or
saving a large subspace.

{\bf Preconditioning IRK Methods for Time-Dependent Fluid Flow Problems}}

Department of Mathematics \& Statistics \\ Texas Tech University \\ Lubbock \\ TX 79407

We examine block preconditioners for time-dependent incompressible
Navier-Stokes problems and some related coupled problems. In some
time-dependent problems, explicit time stepping methods can require much
smaller time steps for stability than is needed for reasonable accuracy.
This leads to taking many more time steps than would otherwise be needed.
With implicit time stepping methods, we can take larger steps, but at the
price of needing to solve large linear systems at each time step. We
consider implicit Runge-Kutta (IRK) methods. Suppose our PDE has been
linearized and discretized with $N$ degrees of freedom. Using an
$s$-staghe IRK method leads to an $s N \times s N$ linear system that
must be solved at each time step. These linear systems are block $s \times
s$ systems, where each block is $N \times N$. We investigate
preconditioners for such systems, where we take advantage of the
structure of the subblocks.

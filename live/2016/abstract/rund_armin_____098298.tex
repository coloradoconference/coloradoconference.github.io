{\bf PDE-constrained optimization with switching controls}}

Karl Kunisch\\
Christian Clason\end{center}

The talk is concerned with optimal control problems for parabolic
differential equations subject to switching penalties. Based on sparse
control we formulate convex and nonconvex penalty functions that promote
switching between different control components. After suitable
regularizations both problems can be solved using semismooth
Newton-Krylov methods with adjoint-based exact first and second
derivatives. The convex and nonconvex formulations are compared in
numerical experiments.

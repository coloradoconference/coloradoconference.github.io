We combine two algorithmic techniques for solving systems of linear
inequalities, the relaxation method of Agmon, Motzkin et al. and the
randomized Kaczmarz method. In doing so, we obtain a family of algorithms
that generalize and extend both techniques. While we prove similar
convergence results, our computational experiments show our algorithms
often vastly outperform the original methods.

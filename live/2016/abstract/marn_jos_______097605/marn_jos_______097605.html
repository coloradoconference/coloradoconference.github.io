Different strategies have been proposed when the skew-symmetric part has exactly rank . In [1] it is presented a progressive GMRES method that allows for the short-term computation of an orthogonal Krylov subspace basis. As pointed out in [5], although the method is mathematically equivalent to full GMRES [6] in practice it may suffer from instabilities due to the loss of orthogonality between the vectors of the generated Krylov subspace basis. In the same paper, the authors propose a Schur complement method that also permits the application of short-term formulas.

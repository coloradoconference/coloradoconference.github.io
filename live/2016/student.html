STUDENT REGISTRATION SUPPORT

Lodging and conference expenses may be awarded, subject to NSF funding. We cannot guarantee any student funding at this time. Student paper winners will have priority should funds become available.

Postdocs are not considered "students" for this competition, nor are they eligible for student registration fees.

Students seeking support should email the conference coordinator, Annette Anthony for more information.

SUBMITTING PAPERS FOR STUDENT PAPER COMPETITION

Any student or new Ph.D. whose degree was awarded after Jan 1, 2015 is eligible to submit a paper. Papers must contain original unpublished research due mainly to the student. These papers must be received before January 23, 2016. The student must be the primary author and the paper may be no more than 10 pages in length.

The paper must be emailed as an attachment in PDF format to annettefanthony@gmail.com and identified in the email as an entry for the 2016 student paper competition. In addition to emailing the paper, an abstract for the paper must be submitted through our Abstract Submission form. Sending only an abstract is unacceptable. A panel of judges will determine the best papers.

Additional information on submitting a student paper for the competition can be found here

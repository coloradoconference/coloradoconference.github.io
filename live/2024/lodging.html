18th Copper Mountain Conference On

Iterative Methods

Cambria Hotel Copper Mountain


Located in Center Village within walking distance to not only the Conference Center meeting space but activities, restaurants and shopping. Double Queen hotel rooms are available as are Double Queen Junior suites, with a little sitting area with a pull-out sleeper sofa.

Per night rates are as follows

        Double Queen Room: $185 (before taxes and fees)
        Double Queen Suite: $245 (before taxes and fees)

Make your room reservation directly with Cambria early. We have a block of rooms reserved for the conference which is released 30 days before the start of the conference. In some previous years, we have indeed run out of rooms, so please reserve your rooms as soon as possible.

State and local taxes (16.275%) will be added to the cost of the room. Attendees will be responsible making their own reservations. Please call the Cambria Hotel reservation line at (970) 306-7907, and identify yourself as a member of the CU Math Conference, with group code 6114 in order to get the special rates, or book online:

https://www.choicehotels.com/reservations/groups/VO77O6

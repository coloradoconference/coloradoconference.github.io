22nd Copper Mountain Conference On

Multigrid Methods

Cambria Hotel Copper Mountain

Located in Center Village within walking distance to not only the Conference Center meeting space but activities, restaurants and shopping. Double Queen and King hotel rooms are available.

Per night rates are as follows

        Double Queen hotel room: $189        King hotel room: $169

Make your room reservation directly with Cambria early. We have a block of rooms reserved for the conference which is released 30 days before the start of the conference. In some previous years, we have indeed run out of rooms, so please reserve your rooms as soon as possible.

State and local taxes (16.275%) will be added to the cost of the room. Attendees will be responsible making their own reservations. Please call the Cambria Hotel reservation line at (970) 406-7565, and identify yourself as a member of the CU Math Conference, with group code 6503 (CU Math) in order to get the special rates, or book online here.

Other lodging options

While conference rates are only available for the hotel rooms above, there are many other lodging options at Copper Mountain, including through VRBO, Airbnb, and Copper Mountain Resort Lodging. Note, however, that some of these bookings will require you to pick up keys somewhere other than Copper Mountain -- please be sure that you understand both where your lodging is and where you need to get keys, and that you have transportation available to get to both places.
